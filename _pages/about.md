Hello, I am Talha. My primary research interests center on optimal and equitable resource planning in healthcare systems. Specifically, my research focuses on:

1. **Healthcare Operations Management:** Healthcare financing and payment systems, resource allocation, and the effects of healthcare policies on provider decisions, service availability, and patient access.
2. **Digital Health:** Wearable technologies, AI-enabled decision support, and the effects of digital health interventions on patient behavior, healthcare delivery, and operational outcomes.

I earned an M.Sc. in Business Analytics from The University of Tulsa under the supervision of <a href="https://utulsa.edu/people/wen-chyuan-chiang/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wen-Chyuan Chiang</a> and <a href="https://utulsa.edu/people/ismail-abdulrashid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Ismail Abdulrashid</a>.

## Research Overview

* Empirical healthcare operations and service systems
* Data-driven patient flow and capacity management
* Empirical analysis of healthcare resource allocation under uncertainty
* Data-driven treatment decisions and patient prioritization
* Predictive analytics and AI for operational decision-making
* Interpretable and reliable clinical decision-support systems
